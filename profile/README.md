## About Us

**We The People RP** is a whitelisted FiveM roleplay community focused on meaningful interactions, long-term character development, and stories shaped by player decisions.

This organization contains the resources, services, integrations, and internal tooling developed to support the WTPRP community.

## What We Build

* Custom FiveM resources and gameplay systems
* Player-owned business and economy features
* Police, EMS, government, and civilian systems
* Community websites, applications, and administrative tools
* Server infrastructure, integrations, and developer utilities
* Performance improvements, bug fixes, and quality-of-life updates

## Our Development Philosophy

We build systems specifically around the needs of our community rather than relying entirely on generic, recycled solutions.

Our priorities are:

* **Immersive gameplay** that supports serious roleplay
* **Reliable systems** designed for a live production environment
* **Meaningful progression** without unnecessary handouts
* **Consistent enforcement** and fair gameplay
* **Original features** created specifically for WTPRP

## Community

WTPRP is built around quality roleplay, accountable staff, active departments, a functioning economy, and a community where characters and their decisions matter.
